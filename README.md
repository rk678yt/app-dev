# SENNA
**A documentary about the iconic driver named Ayrton Senna**
*The documentary starts with the footage of Monaco 1984, where many drivers crash or slow down except for Senna himself*
>If you no longer go for a gap that exists, you're no longer a racing driver.

---

*Ayrton Senna's F1 career consist of 4 teams he raced with*
1. Toleman (1984)
2. Lotus (1985-1987)
3. McLaren (1988-1993)
4. Williams (1994)

---

*Notable F1 events shown in the documentary film*
- Iconic Monaco 1984 race where Senna almost got his first F1 win, but finish as 2nd place after his lap with the overtake was not rercorded.
- His actual first win came in the 1985 Portugese Grand Prix, where it rained so hard, but Senna was unbothered.
- His World Driver's Championship title fights in McLaren, which result in controversies and almost getting banned
- The move to Williams, where he tried to win another WDC, but resulted in his death in 1994 at Imola.
