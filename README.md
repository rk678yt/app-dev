# SENNA
**A documentary about the iconic driver named Ayrton Senna**

*The documentary starts with the footage of the 1984 race in Monaco, where many drivers either crash or slow down except for Senna himself*
>If you no longer go for a gap that exists, you're no longer a racing driver.

~~The GOAT of F1 racing~~

	Rivals faced in the film: Alain Prost, Nigel Mansell, Michael schumacher (has his own documentary film).

### Ayrton Senna's F1 career consist of 4 teams he raced with
1. Toleman
2. Lotus
3. McLaren (~3~ WDC)
4. Williams

| Team     | Year |
| :---        |    :----:   |
| Toleman     | 1984       |
| Lotus   | 1985-1987        |
| McLaren      | 1989-1993       |
| Williams   | 1994        |

### Notable F1 events shown in the documentary film
- Iconic Monaco 1984 race where Senna almost got his first F1 win, but finished 2nd place after his lap with the final overtake was not rercorded.
- exclusive onboard footages, most notably his monaco 1989 lap.
- Controversial world driver's championship battles.
- Senna's death in ^1994^. 😞
